Computational biology · Pohang, Korea

I analyze and integrate single-cell RNA and chromatin data across tissues to understand how epithelia respond to injury and regenerate.

I have integrated more than fifty human single-cell datasets to map injury-response programs that are shared across barrier tissues and those specific to one lineage. With paired scRNA-seq and scATAC-seq, I infer the gene regulatory networks behind gastric regeneration and stem cell memory. In co-first-author studies of the stomach, intestine and human tongue, I used single-cell transcriptomics to show how adult epithelial stem cells hold a reserve state, drive regeneration after injury, restore homeostasis, and divide their output between distinct cell lineages.
